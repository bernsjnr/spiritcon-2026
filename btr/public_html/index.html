For all people and all paces.

Bully the Roads is a grassroots community sports club in London. Running comes first, but it's never only about the run.

First 5k or fiftieth marathon, you'll find a group moving at your speed and people who'll still be there at the finish. No fees, no egos, nobody left behind.

Join the community

What we do

Three ways in

  • Run

    Weekly group runs with pace groups and a sweeper, so every speed has company.

  • Track + Play

    Drills, relays and reps on the track. Competitive if you want it, playful if you don't.

  • Social

    Coffee, cheer stations and nights out. The bit that keeps people coming back.
