Who we are

Bully the Roads ("BTR", "we", "us") is a grassroots community sports club based in London. We're the data controller for the personal data described here.

Controller: CONFIRM: legal name, and whether BTR is an unincorporated club, CIC or company. Contact: bullytheroads@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you apply to join

  • Your name, email address and mobile number.
  • Roughly where you live, as an area or postcode district. Never your full address.
  • Your Instagram or Strava handle, if you choose to share it.
  • Your running level, comfortable pace, goals, target times and events you're training for.
  • Why you want to join, how you heard about us and when you can usually run.
  • Your consent and whether you'd like our emails, with the date and time you gave them.

To keep the form safe

  • Your IP address and the time you submitted, so we can limit repeat submissions and spot spam.
  • A bot check from Cloudflare Turnstile, which looks at technical signals from your browser.

If you win a challenge

Your first name, surname initial and result, which we show on the Challenges page. Challenges run on Strava. We don't connect to Strava or pull any of your Strava data into this site.

We don't ask for health information. If you choose to tell us something like that in a free-text box, we'll only use it to look after you at our events.

You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing us. It won't affect anything we did before you withdrew it.

Who sees it

Only the BTR organisers who review applications can see your application. We never sell your data or share it for anyone else's marketing.

We use a small number of service providers to run the site. They process data for us, under contract, and only as we instruct:

  • Hostinger hosts this website, its database and our email. CONFIRM: data centre region, choose UK or EU in hPanel
  • Google (Gmail) receives notification emails that include your application, sent to our club inbox.
  • Cloudflare runs the Turnstile bot check on the join form.

Once you join the WhatsApp community, WhatsApp (Meta) handles your number under its own privacy policy, and other members of the community may be able to see your number and name. Strava works the same way for the club and challenges.

Some providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they rely on UK safeguards such as adequacy regulations or the ICO's International Data Transfer Agreement.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the data we hold about you
  • correct anything that's wrong
  • delete your data
  • restrict or object to how we use it
  • send your data to you, or someone else, in a common format

It's free, and we'll reply within one month.

Deleting your data

Email bullytheroads@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data", ideally from the address you applied with so we can find you. We'll delete your application and email you when it's done. If you're in the WhatsApp community, we'll remove you from that too.

Cookies and tracking

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking scripts. Public pages don't set cookies.

The only cookie is a strictly necessary session cookie for BTR organisers when they sign in to manage the site. Cloudflare Turnstile runs only on the join form, to tell people from bots.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, tell us first and we'll try to put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

If we change this notice we'll update the date at the top, and tell members about anything significant.
