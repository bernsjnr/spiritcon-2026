/ About

More
Than An
Event.

To quiet the noise, slow down, and rediscover a sacred rhythm in your everyday life — SPIRITCon 2026 is for you.

It's a space to pause, listen, and be renewed. A gathering for those who are hungry for something real. For those who want to hear God again, not just in moments, but in the flow of their lives.

Across two days, you'll step into an atmosphere of worship, creativity, and deep formation designed to help you reconnect, refocus, and experience transformation from the inside out.

If you've been craving clarity…
If you're seeking depth…
If you're ready for a fresh move of God in your life…

Join us. May 22–23, 2026.

With hundreds of worshippers and creatives from around the world, featuring guest speakers and worship leaders.

/ What to Expect

Deep Worship

An atmosphere intentionally crafted to slow you down and draw you in. Not performance — encounter.

Honest Teaching

Speakers who will challenge and equip you. Real conversations about faith, formation, and life with God.

Real Community

Hundreds of worshippers and creatives from around the world, gathered around one thing. Come as you are.
